About the App

Business Billing – Invoice Maker is a complete business management solution designed for shopkeepers, freelancers, and small businesses. Create invoices and receipts in seconds, manage customers, track payments, and view business reports easily.

Key Features

  • Create professional invoices & receipts quickly
  • Customer & payment management
  • Track pending and received payments
  • Product & stock management
  • Download reports in Excel
  • Simple, fast, and user-friendly design
  • Works offline
