About the App

Business Billing – Invoice Maker is a powerful yet simple billing solution designed for shopkeepers, freelancers, and small business owners. Create professional invoices and receipts in seconds, manage customers, track payments, and analyze your business with ease.

Key Features

  • Quick invoice & receipt generation
  • Customer & payment management
  • Track pending and received payments
  • Product and stock management
  • Export reports in Excel format
  • Offline support
  • Clean, fast, and easy-to-use interface
